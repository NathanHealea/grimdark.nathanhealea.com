# How to Edit Your Profile

You can update your profile at any time from [Profile Settings](/profile/edit).

## What You Can Change

### Avatar

Upload or replace your profile picture. This appears next to your name throughout the site — on the member directory, battle reports, and leaderboard.

### Display Name

Change how your name appears across the league. Must be 2-50 characters and unique — no two players can share the same name.

### Bio

Add or update an optional bio (up to 500 characters) to tell other players about yourself. Supports basic markdown formatting.

### Factions

Manage the armies you play using the faction picker. The picker shows the full hierarchy:

- **Alliance** (Imperium, Chaos, Xenos)
  - **Faction** (Space Marines, Aeldari, etc.)
    - **Sub-faction** (Blood Angels, Dark Angels, etc.)

Your faction list is used when [joining seasons](/guides/joining-a-season) and [submitting battle reports](/guides/submitting-a-battle-report).

### Password

If you signed up with email, you can change your password from the bottom of the settings page. Enter your new password and confirm it (minimum 6 characters).

## Saving

Click **Save Changes** to apply your updates.

> If you need to change your league role or other admin-level settings, contact an organizer.

# How to Create a Battle Report 

Admins can submit battle reports on behalf of any league members. This is useful when players forget to log their own games or when recording results from organized events.

## Steps

### 1. Navigate to the Submit Page

Go to [Submit Battle Report](/battle-reports/submit) from the main menu, or from **Admin > Battle Reports** click the submit link.

### 2. Fill in Game Details

- **Event Date** — When the game was played (cannot be in the future)
- **Season** — Which league season this game counts toward. As an admin, you can assign reports to any season — members are restricted to the current season only.
- **Mission** — The mission played from the official mission deck
- **Deployment** — The deployment map used
- **Battle Points** — The points size: Combat Patrol (500), Incursion (1000), Strike Force (2000), or Onslaught (3000)
- **Rounds Played** — How many rounds were completed (1-5)

### 3. Fill in Attacker and Defender

For each side:

- **Player** — Select the member from the dropdown
- **Faction** — The army they played (filtered to that player's profile factions)
- **Score** — Their final victory points
- **Outcome** — Win, Loss, or Draw
- **Units Lost** (optional) — How many units were destroyed
- **Models Lost** (optional) — Total models removed
- **Tabled** (optional) — Check if the player was completely wiped out

> The attacker and defender must be different players.

### 4. Add Round Stats (Optional)

Click **Add Round** to record per-round details for more granular tracking. Each round captures points earned, units lost, and models lost for both sides. Up to 5 rounds can be added.

### 5. Choose Draft or Published

- **Draft** — Save to finish later. Only visible to the reporting player and admins. Partial data is allowed.
- **Published** — Finalize the report. All required fields must be filled. The report is visible to everyone and counts toward standings.

### 6. Submit

Click **Submit Battle Report**. You'll be redirected to the battle reports feed.

## Admin Privileges

Compared to regular members, admins can:

- Assign battle reports to **any season** (not just the current one)
- Submit reports for **any two players** in the league
- Edit and delete reports submitted by others (see [Editing a Battle Report](/guides/editing-a-battle-report) and [Deleting a Battle Report](/guides/deleting-a-battle-report))

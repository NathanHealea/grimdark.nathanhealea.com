# How to Join a Season

Ready to join the fight? Here's how to sign up for a league season and declare your army.

## Prerequisites

Before you can join a season, you need:

1. **A Grimdark League account** — [Sign up](/sign-up) if you haven't already
2. **At least one faction on your profile** — Add your factions in [profile settings](/profile/edit)

## Steps

### 1. Find the Season

Navigate to the [Seasons](/seasons) page from the main menu. You'll see a list of all current and upcoming seasons. Click on the season you want to join.

### 2. Pick Your Faction

On the season detail page, you'll see a **Join Season** section. Select the faction you want to play this season from the dropdown. This list is filtered to the factions you've added to your profile.

> If you don't see a faction you want to play, head to [profile settings](/profile/edit) and add it first.

### 3. Confirm

Click **Join Season** and you're in. Your name and chosen faction will appear on the season roster.

## Changing Your Army

Already joined but want to switch factions? On the season detail page:

1. Click **Change Army**
2. Select your new faction from the dropdown
3. Click **Save**

## Leaving a Season

If you need to drop out, click **Leave Season** on the season detail page. You can rejoin later as long as the season is still open.

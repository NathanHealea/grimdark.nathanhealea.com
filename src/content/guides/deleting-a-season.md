# How to Delete a Season

Deleting a season removes it permanently. Battle reports linked to the season are **not deleted** — they are unlinked and become unassigned.

## Steps

### Option A: From the Seasons List

1. Go to **Admin > Seasons**
2. Click the actions menu (three dots) on the season
3. Select **Delete Season**
4. Confirm the deletion

### Option B: From the Edit Page

1. Open the season's edit page
2. Scroll to the **Danger Zone** section at the bottom
3. Click **Delete Season**
4. Confirm the deletion

## What Happens

- The season is permanently removed
- All battle reports that were assigned to this season have their season reference cleared (they become unassigned)
- Roster entries for this season are deleted
- Leaderboard data for this season is no longer available

> This action cannot be undone. If you want to temporarily hide a season, set its status to **Draft** instead.

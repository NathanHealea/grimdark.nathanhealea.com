# How to Delete a Battle Report

Only admins can delete battle reports. This permanently removes the report and all associated round stats.

## Steps

1. Go to **Admin > Battle Reports**
2. Find the report you want to delete
3. Click the actions menu on the report row
4. Select **Delete Report**
5. Confirm the deletion

## What Happens

- The battle report and all its round stats are permanently removed
- If the report was published, standings and leaderboard rankings are recalculated without it
- The deletion cannot be undone

> Before deleting, consider whether editing the report would be more appropriate. If the issue is incorrect data, [editing the report](/guides/editing-a-battle-report) preserves the record while fixing the mistake.

# How to Manage a Season Roster

As an admin, you can manually add or remove members from a season's roster and change their selected faction.

## Adding a Member to the Roster

1. Go to **Admin > Seasons** and click **Edit Season** on the season you want to manage
2. Scroll to the **Roster** section
3. Select a **Profile** from the dropdown — only profiles not already on the roster will appear
4. Select the **Faction** they'll play — this dropdown is filtered to the factions on that player's profile
5. Click **Add**

The member will immediately appear on the roster and in the season's leaderboard.

> If the player you want to add doesn't appear in the dropdown, they may already be on the roster, or they may not have any factions on their profile yet.

## Changing a Member's Faction

1. On the roster table in the edit page, click the **faction name** next to the member
2. Select the new faction from the dropdown
3. Click **Save**

## Removing a Member from the Roster

1. On the roster table, click **Remove** next to the member
2. Confirm the removal

The member's entry is removed from the roster. Their battle reports for the season are **not affected** — they remain linked to the season.

## Notes

- Members can also join, change army, or leave a season themselves from the public season detail page
- The roster determines who appears on the season leaderboard
- A member must have at least one faction on their profile before they can be added to a roster

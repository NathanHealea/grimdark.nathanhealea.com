# How to Edit a Season

You can update any season's details, change its status, and manage its roster after creation.

## Steps

### 1. Open the Edit Page

From **Admin > Seasons**, click the actions menu (three dots) on the season you want to edit, then select **Edit Season**. You can also click **Edit Season** from the season detail page if you're an admin.

### 2. Update Season Details

All the same fields from creation are available:

- **Season Name**, **Start Date**, **End Date**, **Battle Size**, **Description**, **Rules & Regulations**, **Status**

> The season number is displayed but cannot be changed.

### 3. Save Changes

Click **Update Season** to save. The same validation rules apply — end date must be after start date, and published seasons cannot overlap.

# How to Change Your Season Army

Already joined a season but want to switch factions? You can change your army at any time while the season is active.

## Steps

### 1. Go to the Season Page

Navigate to [Seasons](/seasons) and click on the season you're in.

### 2. Change Your Faction

In the roster section, you'll see your current faction displayed. Click **Change Army** to enter edit mode.

### 3. Select a New Faction

Choose your new faction from the dropdown. This list is filtered to the factions on your profile.

> If the faction you want isn't listed, add it to your profile first in [Profile Settings](/profile/edit).

### 4. Save

Click **Save** to confirm the change. Your updated faction will appear on the season roster immediately.

## Leaving a Season

If you need to drop out entirely, click **Leave Season** on the season detail page. You can rejoin later as long as the season is still active.

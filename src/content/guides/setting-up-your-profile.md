# How to Set Up Your Profile

Your profile is your identity in the Grimdark League. Here's how to get set up.

## Creating Your Account

1. Go to the [Sign Up](/sign-up) page
2. Enter your email and a password (minimum 6 characters), or sign up with **Discord**
3. After signing up, you'll be taken to the profile setup page

## Initial Profile Setup

When you first create your account, you'll set up your profile:

- **Display Name** (required) — This is how other players will see you. Must be 2-50 characters and unique across the league.
- **Factions** (optional) — Select the armies you play. You can always add more later.

## Editing Your Profile

Visit [Profile Settings](/profile/edit) to update your profile at any time.

### Avatar

Upload a profile picture to represent yourself. This appears next to your name throughout the site.

### Display Name

Change your display name. The same uniqueness rules apply — no two players can share a name.

### Bio

Add an optional bio (up to 500 characters) to tell other players about yourself. Supports basic markdown formatting.

### Factions

Manage the factions you play. The faction picker shows the full hierarchy:

- **Alliance** (Imperium, Chaos, Xenos)
  - **Faction** (Space Marines, Aeldari, etc.)
    - **Sub-faction** (Blood Angels, Dark Angels, etc.)

Select as many as you like. Your faction list is used when joining seasons and submitting battle reports.

### Password

If you signed up with email, you can change your password from the profile settings page. Enter your new password and confirm it.

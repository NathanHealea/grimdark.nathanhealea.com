# How to Create a Season

Seasons are time-bounded league periods that organize battle reports and standings. Here's how to create one.

## Steps

### 1. Navigate to Season Management

Go to **Admin > Seasons** from the admin menu, then click **Create Season** in the top right.

### 2. Fill in Season Details

- **Season Name** (optional) — A thematic name like "The Crusade Begins". If left blank, the season will just display as "Season #".
- **Start Date** (required) — When the season begins.
- **End Date** (required) — When the season ends. Must be after the start date.
- **Battle Size** (required) — The points level for games this season: Combat Patrol (500), Incursion (1000), Strike Force (2000), or Onslaught (3000).
- **Description** (optional) — A short summary of the season.
- **Rules & Regulations** (optional) — Detailed season rules. Supports markdown formatting.

### 3. Set the Status

- **Draft** — The season is not visible to members. Use this while you're still setting things up.
- **Published** — The season is live and visible to everyone. Members can join the roster and submit battle reports.

> Published seasons cannot overlap with other published seasons. If you get an overlap error, check your dates or set the other season to draft first.

### 4. Backfill Battle Reports (Optional)

Check **Backfill existing battle reports** to automatically assign any unassigned battle reports that fall within the season's date range. This is useful when creating a season after games have already been played.

> Backfill is only available when creating a season, not when editing.

### 5. Submit

Click **Create Season**. The season will be assigned the next sequential number automatically. You'll be redirected to the seasons list.
